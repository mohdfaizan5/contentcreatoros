 7 high-performing Twitter/X hook templates tailored for company accounts
(designed to instantly stop the scroll and drive engagement.)


## 1. The "Before & After" (Value-Driven)

Template:
```
Before [doing X], we used to [struggle
with old method].
Now, using [our product/feature], we
[achieve amazing result].
Here is exactly how we did it in [Number]
steps:

[Step 1]

[Step 2]

[Step 3]

```
example

```
Before using automation, we used to
manually track every software bug in
spreadsheets.
Now, using our new dashboard, we save 15
hours every single week.
Here is exactly how we did it in 3 steps:

Auto-tagging incoming support
tickets

Syncing Slack alerts directly to Jira

Setting up automated weekly team
recaps
```

## 2. The Contrarian / Myth-Buster

Template:
```
Everyone thinks [common industry belief]
is the best way to [achieve goal].
It’s actually a massive waste of time.
Here is what you should do instead to [get
better result]:
[Actionable tip or insight]2
```
example

```
Everyone thinks posting 5 times a day is
the best way to grow your brand on X.
It’s actually a massive waste of time.
Here is what you should do instead to get
better results:
Write 2 high-value, deeply researched
threads per week and engage under large
industry accounts. Quality beats noise.
```


## 3. The Curated Resource List

Template:
```
[Number] [tools/resources/books] that
helped our team [achieve a specific win]
this year:

[Name] - [1-sentence benefit]

[Name] - [1-sentence benefit]
Save this for your [workflow/project]
this week! 3
```
example
```
5 Chrome extensions that helped our
marketing team launch our Q2 campaign
in half the time:

Loom - For lightning-fast video
feedback

Grammarly - To keep copy mistakefree

ColorZilla - For instant brand color
matching

Milanote - For digital brainstorming
boards

Toggle Track - To optimize our daily
workflow
Save this for your next project launch!

```


## 4. The "Behind-The-Scenes" Process

Template:
```
We just built/launched [new feature/
product].
Instead of just showing the polished
result, here is the messy behind-thescenes of how we made it happen:
[Thread format] 👇 4
```

example
```
We just launched our new mobile app
workspace.
Instead of just showing the polished app
store screens, here is the messy behindthe-scenes of how our design team
scrapped 14 iterations before getting it
right:
[Thread format] 👇
```


## 5. The Framework / Step-By-Step Guide

Template:
```
Want to [achieve a desired outcome]?
Steal our exact [Number]-step
framework:
1. The Setup: [Action]
2️. The Execution: [Action]
3️. The Result: [Action]4
```
example
```
Want to write email newsletters that
actually get opened?
Steal our exact 3-step framework:
1️. The Setup: Write 5 different subject
lines using active verbs.
2️. The Execution: Keep your preview text
under 40 characters to avoid clipping.
3️. The Result: Deliver one clear, single callto-action button at the very end.

```

## 6. The Hard Data / Case Study

Template:
```
How we helped our client [achieve
impressive metric, e.g., increase
conversion by 30%] in just [Number]
days:
📉 The Problem: [1 sentence summary]
📈 The Solution: [1 sentence summary]
🚀 The Result: [Data point]

Read the full breakdown here: [Link] 6
```
example
```
How we helped a fintech startup
decrease checkout abandonment by 42%
in just 14 days:
📉 The Problem: A confusing 4-page
checkout flow.
📈 The Solution: Consolidated everything
into a secure single-page checkout.
🚀 The Result: Sales jumped $12,000 in
the first week.
Read the full breakdown here: [Link]
```


## 7. The Community Question (Engagement-Bait)

Template:
```
[Number] years in [Industry] taught us
one universal truth:
[Bold statement or unpopular opinion].
What is your biggest hot take on this? Let
us know below. 👇 7
```
example
```
10 years in B2B software development
taught us one universal truth:

developers who use AI will replace those
who don't.
What is your biggest hot take on this? Let
us know below. 👇

```
